svaRhythm Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

svaRhythm is a library for listening, watching, and reading. You do not create an account, and you do not sign in.

What the app stores on your phone

Favorites, the item you last opened, playback position, and the PDF page you were on stay on this phone. They are not sent to an account, because there is no account.

What the app sends

When you open an audio item or a PDF, the app adds one to that item’s view count. The count is a single number on the item. It is not tied to your name, email, or device.

The library itself (audio, video, PDFs, and calendar events) is loaded from our server so the app can show it.

Classes that play from YouTube are loaded from YouTube. YouTube’s own privacy policy applies to that playback.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or payment information. We do not use advertising, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Children

The app can be used without an account. It is not directed at collecting personal information from children.
